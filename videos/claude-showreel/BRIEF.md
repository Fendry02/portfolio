## Intent

A 30-second motion-design showreel, framed as Claude's own résumé reel, for the
portfolio. User's words: "make a dynamic 30-second motion graphics video that shows
what an incredible motion designer you are, like it's your showreel for a résumé.
go all out for the portfolio."

## Customizations

- Unnarrated, beat-driven: an original 128 BPM track synthesized offline (16 bars = 30.0s),
  scene cuts on bar lines, hits on beats.
- Bundled SFX (whoosh, glitch, impact, pop) layered on seams.
- Each scene demonstrates a different craft: squash & stretch, kinetic type, easing /
  graph editor, grid pattern, 3D camera, data viz, fast-cut range burst, logo lockup.
- Persistent reel HUD: timecode, frame counter, scene index, crop marks.
